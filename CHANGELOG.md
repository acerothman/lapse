# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- A focus timer in the menu bar. A timer icon while idle. A dot and the countdown during a focus or a break.
- During a focus, the apps you turned on hide or quit, and a blocked site or app in front is covered. They come back on the break.
- Ending early takes a hold or a short reason. Pause keeps the block up.
- Settings is a native window with General, Blocking, Sessions, and Tracking. Cards and labels use system colors in light and dark mode.
- Tracking stays: daily limits, the limit screen, focus hours, and feed-only counting for X, Instagram, YouTube, and Reddit. A focus still blocks the whole site.
- Shortcuts can start a session, end one (with the prompt), pause tracking, read today's time, and set a limit. A Focus can start a session or turn the tracking rules on.
- Opening a blocked app or site can wait a few seconds, or ask why, when you turn that on. The update check still reads GitHub releases.

## 1.1.0

- Track Mac apps you pick. Time counts while the app is in front, and the icon comes from the app.
- A calm screen when a site or app passes its limit. Snooze 5 minutes, snooze 15 minutes, or turn it off for the day. Settings can turn the screen off.
- A daily limit for each site and each app, next to the overall limit. The menu bar ring still follows the overall limit.
- Focus hours on the days and times you set. A stricter limit during that window, or a block for the whole window.
- On launch, and once a day, Lapse checks GitHub for a newer release. The popover says so, with a download button. Check for updates is in the menu too.

## 1.0.0

- Welcome window on first launch: sites, daily limit, browser access, notifications, and open at login. Open it again from Settings.
- Popover with today's total, time and visits per site, and a chart of the last seven days.
- Menu bar ring that fills through the day and turns red past the daily limit.
- Site logo and a session timer while a tracked site is in front.
- Icon fetch for sites you add, cached on the Mac.
- Daily limit, with a notification at 80% and another when the limit is reached.
- Open at login.
