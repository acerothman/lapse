# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Track Mac apps you pick. Time counts while the app is in front, and the icon comes from the app.
- A calm screen when a site or app passes its limit. Snooze 5 minutes, snooze 15 minutes, or turn it off for the day. Settings can turn the screen off.
- A daily limit for each site and each app, next to the overall limit. The menu bar ring still follows the overall limit.
- Focus hours on the days and times you set. A stricter limit during that window, or a block for the whole window.
- Check for updates in the menu bar and the popover. Lapse also checks on its own.

## 1.0.0

- Welcome window on first launch: sites, daily limit, browser access, notifications, and open at login. Open it again from Settings.
- Popover with today's total, time and visits per site, and a chart of the last seven days.
- Menu bar ring that fills through the day and turns red past the daily limit.
- Site logo and a session timer while a tracked site is in front.
- Icon fetch for sites you add, cached on the Mac.
- Daily limit, with a notification at 80% and another when the limit is reached.
- Open at login.
