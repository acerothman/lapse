# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Welcome window on first launch: sites, daily limit, browser access, notifications, and open at login. Open it again from Settings.
- Popover with today's total, time and visits per site, and a chart of the last seven days.
- Menu bar ring that fills through the day and turns red past the daily limit.
- Site logo and a session timer while a tracked site is in front.
- Icon fetch for sites you add, cached on the Mac.
- Daily limit, with a notification at 80% and another when the limit is reached.
- Open at login.
